Sanad Hub

Sanad is a private, personal productivity tool used by a single individual to read and organize files in their own Google Drive, Google Docs and Google Sheets.

It is not offered to the public, and no other users can sign in.

Privacy Policy · Contact: github.com/teamy3690-collab
